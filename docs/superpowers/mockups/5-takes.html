How it works

  • Beats = the AI clusters every repeat of a line together (you already have this clustering in the backend).
  • One click on a take's ◯ makes it the winner; all other takes in that beat auto-cut.
  • Each take is tagged clean / false start / stumble / repeat and a ▶ to A/B them instantly.
  • Coverage at a glance: green "picked", amber "pick one" / "no clean take".
  • Winner still editable — "fine-trim in Edit" jumps to that span in the word editor to shave its edges.
  • Build · 1:58 shows the final runtime from your picks.

Why it fits your footage

  • ~50 word-run cuts → ~8 best-take decisions.
  • Same keep/cut data as the Edit tab — flip between the two views freely.
